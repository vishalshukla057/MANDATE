## Brand & Style
The brand targets private wealth managers, family offices, and sovereign capital allocators who prioritize enduring stability over fleeting fintech trends. The interface evokes the tactile weight of bond certificates, bespoke heavy-gauge stationery, and bespoke leather desk blotters. 

The aesthetic is an editorial fusion of British private banking discipline and modern architectural minimalism. It explicitly avoids generic tech gloss, neon accents, and algorithmic homogeneity. Surfaces feel deliberate, grounded, and physical—constructed with calculated proportions, fine hairline boundaries, and restrained metallic warmth. Interaction design emphasizes permanence and precision: actions settle with decisive weight rather than float or bounce.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in deep ink-black and British racing / botanical emerald, illuminated by brushed champagne gold:

- **Primary (`#0D5C3A` & `#064E3B`)**: Pure botanical emerald. Evokes heavy architectural marble, banker’s lamps, and sovereign reserves. Used for verified status badges, primary commitments, yield signals, and structural emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#CBB78D` with highlights in `#DFD0AD`, `#F5ECD5`, `#F7F4EA`)**: Warm champagne and burnished ivory gold. Provides luminous contrast against deep backgrounds, serving as secondary interaction indicators, key financial figures, rule dividers, and bespoke badges.
- **Tertiary (`#064E3B`)**: Deepest pine emerald. Deployed for subtle tinted container backgrounds, selected state backdrops, and pressed card surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#090D16` and `#0F172A`)**: Saturated fountain pen ink-black. Serves as the bedrock canvas, replacing sterile cold grays with deep, rich midnight blue-black undertones that convey permanence and optical density.

## Typography
Typography creates deliberate tension between high-society editorial heritage and clinical mathematical rigor:

- **Headlines & Display (`Newsreader`)**: Set with optical sizing and delicate serifs. Display figures convey executive authority, reminiscent of bespoke financial prospectuses and treaty documents. Italic styles are reserved strictly for qualitative subheadings or market commentary quotes.
- **Body & Structural Text (`Manrope`)**: A clean, balanced neo-grotesque that ensures crystalline legibility for complex contracts, portfolio allocation narratives, and operational audit trails.
- **Data, Ledgers & Metadata (`Space Mono`)**: Used strictly for financial figures, tickers, ISIN codes, transaction hashes, and micro-labels. Always rendered in tabular, uppercase, or calibrated spacing to reinforce ledger veracity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a strict architectural 12-column grid on desktop (8 columns on tablet, 4 on mobile) with generous margins that frame content like a museum catalog.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: Outer margin of `3rem` (`48px`) with gutters of `1.5rem` (`24px`). Negative space is deliberate and structural, allowing dense tabular ledgers to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Outer margin scales to `2rem` (`32px`), preserving column alignment while collapsing secondary contextual panes into drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (Under 768px)**: Edge-to-edge ink canvas with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Multi-column metric displays reflow into single-column vertical summaries connected by hairline golden horizontal rules.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through physical stratification, subtle inner glows, and crisp material rules rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering**: 
  - *Base Floor*: Deep Fountain Pen Ink (`#090D16`).
  - *Elevated Cards/Surfaces*: Ink Slate (`#0F172A`) or deep pine undertone (`#071A12`).
  - *Active / Overlay Panes*: Dense Ink with a 1px inner hairline stroke of Champagne at 15% opacity (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.15)`).
- **Subtle Surface Sheen**: Floating modal surfaces and active trays utilize a subtle multi-stop linear gradient from `rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.04)` at the top down to `rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.98)` at the bottom.
- **Bespoke Borders**: Elements rely on crisp 1px borders using Warm Champagne at 18% opacity (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.18)`), elevating to 40% on hover.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: When floating overlays require elevation, use an ultra-diffused, heavy-weight shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.2)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses minimal, tailored roundness (`roundedness: 1`), embodying hand-cut card stock, stone masonry, and precision craftsmanship.

- Standard buttons, badges, and input containers use `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii.
- Large portfolio cards, sheets, and modal frames use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii maximum.
- Never use pill shapes (`rounded-full`) for interactive buttons; buttons retain crisp corners to convey permanence and weight. Pill shapes are reserved exclusively for quantitative micro-indicators (such as live market status pips).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Execution**: Emerald Green base (`#0D5C3A`) with crisp borders tinted in Champagne (`#CBB78D` at 30% opacity). Text in Champagne White (`#F7F4EA`), styled in `title-md` or `label-lg`. Pressed states deepen to Botanical Slate (`#064E3B`).
- **Secondary / Ledger Action**: Transparent background framed by a 1px border in Champagne (`#CBB78D` at 40% opacity), text in `#DFD0AD`. On hover, the background transitions to `rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive**: Deep crimson wash overlaid onto ink (`#2A0E13`), bordered with muted copper.

### Cards & Ledger Tiles
- Framed in deep ink (`#0F172A`) with a subtle 1px border (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.16)`).
- Internal content is partitioned using horizontal 1px hairline rules instead of empty gaps.
- Card headers pair an editorial serif title (`Newsreader`, italic or regular) with monospaced metadata (`Space Mono`) positioned in the top-right corner.

### Input Fields
- Understated, inset ink surfaces (`#060910`) framed with a 1px border (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.2)`).
- Typography uses `Manrope` for inputs and `Space Mono` for prefix currencies ($ / € / £).
- Focus states apply a crisp, unblurred 1px outer ring in Warm Champagne (`#CBB78D`) and a subtle inner ambient wash of Emerald (`rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.12)`).

### Chips & Badges
- Low-profile status tags featuring 1px borders, uppercase monospace labels (`label-sm`), and a 0.25rem radius.
- "Liquid / Allocated": Emerald tint (`rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.2)`) with Champagne text (`#F5ECD5`).
- "Pending / Escrow": Deep amber-champagne tint (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.12)`) with `#DFD0AD` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp square forms (16px × 16px) with a 2px radius and 1px border (`rgba(203, 183, 141, 0.35)`).
- Checked states fill with Botanical Emerald (`#0D5C3A`) featuring a sharp, champagne-toned checkmark icon.

### Bespoke Financial Ledger & Yield Ticker
- Dense tabular rows with alternate row striping using subtle ink variations (`#090D16` vs `#0C121E`).
- Real-time yield indicators are rendered in `Space Mono`, with micro-indicators using jewel-toned geometric facets (diamonds and carets) rather than generic rounded dots.
